Website Planning Document

Site Name

HappyMart Express

This name reflects a friendly, convenient shopping experience, making it memorable and easy for customers to associate with a retail brand.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to provide customers with store information, product highlights, discount announcements, and a seamless way to explore what HappyMart offers before visiting in person.

Scenarios

  • "What discounts or promotions are available this week at HappyMart?"
  • "Where can I find information about store hours and locations?"

Color Schema

Main Brand Blue: #004aad — Used for headings, borders, and accents.

Warm Accent Orange: #f4a261 — Used for highlights, buttons, and sections requiring attention.

Typography

Primary Font: Arial — Used for all body text and headings for clean readability.
