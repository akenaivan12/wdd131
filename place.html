About Japan

  • Capital: Tokyo
  • Population: 125 million
  • Famous for: Cherry blossoms, temples, technology
  • Region: East Asia
  • Japan has the world’s oldest company Kongo Gumi, founded in 578 AD
  • Square or cube-shaped watermelons are grown as novelty gifts
  • There are “cat islands” such as Tashirojima, where cats outnumber people.
  • Japan is an island nation with 6,800+ islands, though the four main ones (Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu, Shikoku) make up 97% of its land
  • Its capital, Tokyo, is one of the most populous metropolitan areas in the world
  • Japan has the third-largest economy globally by nominal GDP
